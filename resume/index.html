PROJECT EXPERIENCE

Selected Project Experience and Roles

CFX SR 414 Expressway Extension · Orlando, Florida

Led substructure and foundation design for the 58-span elevated viaduct, including hammerhead and inverted-T piers, strut-and-tie and refined finite element checks, and deep foundations designed for challenging loading and soil conditions.

I-70 Floyd Hill · Clear Creek County, Colorado

Led the independent verification of a major cast-in-place post-tensioned segmental bridge, including service and construction-stage behavior, cracked-section stiffness, substructure response, hammerhead piers, twin-leaf piers, and drilled shafts.

Sonovoid Bridge Rehabilitation Program · Broward County, Florida

Led asset evaluation and rehabilitation of precast slab beam bridges, including development and field validation of a near-surface-mounted dowel repair approach to improve lateral load distribution and extend service life.

Roosevelt Bridge Emergency Repair · Stuart, Florida

Reviewed structural behavior and retrofit effectiveness following major cracking, including external post-tensioning, strength recovery, tendon deviators, constructability, bridge jacking, and foundation considerations.

I-395 Signature Bridge · Miami, Florida

Performed detailed analysis and design of precast post-tensioned arch components, cast-in-place starter segments, and supporting end-bent structures for the signature bridge.

Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport Terminal 5 · Broward County, Florida

Led design of a tightly curved four-span steel I-girder flyover bridge, including continuous girders, diaphragms, stiffeners, bearings, reinforced concrete deck, substructures, and driven-pile foundations.

Don Valley Crossing · Toronto, Ontario

Led refined substructure analysis for a balanced-cantilever segmental bridge, including cracked-section stiffness, moment-curvature behavior, secondary effects, and construction- and service-stage reinforcement response.

Queensboro & Throgs Neck Bridges · New York City, New York

Worked on orthotropic deck systems, fatigue and nonlinear analysis, strengthening, seismic vulnerability, bridge retrofits, and customized AASHTO TL-5 railing systems for major long-span bridges.

Henry Hudson Bridge · Bronx, New York

Designed an alternative load-path system to bypass ASR-affected substructure concrete, including micropile-supported foundation modifications, a cast-in-place transfer cap over existing skewbacks, and bridge-level structural analysis.

State Road (S.R.) A1A North Causeway Over ICWW Bridge · St. Lucie County, Florida

Designed bridge substructure and foundations for the high-level fixed bridge replacement, including reinforced concrete piers, oval pile caps, prestressed piles, vessel-collision analysis, scour effects, and soil–structure interaction.

I-4 Ultimate P3 Project · Orlando, Florida

Provided post-design construction support for complex steel I-girder and tub-girder bridges, including shop-drawing review, erection and deck-pour analyses, load rating, shell-element modeling, and strengthening of local stress-concentration regions.

Main Street Bridge Rehabilitation over St. Johns River · Jacksonville, Florida

Supported rehabilitation of the historic vertical-lift bridge over the St. Johns River, with emphasis on structural evaluation and repair of existing bridge components within an active movable-bridge rehabilitation program.

Apollo Bridge Fire-Damage Evaluation and Rehabilitation · Melbourne, Florida

Supported structural evaluation of fire-damaged bridge components and development of rehabilitation measures intended to restore structural capacity, serviceability, and safe operation.

Miami International Airport People Mover Bridge Rehabilitation · Miami, Florida

Developed the finite element model used to evaluate bridge behavior, disk-bearing demands, and jacking forces for bearing replacement while maintaining people-mover operations.

Durham-Orange Light Rail Transit (DOLRT) · Durham, North Carolina

Contributed to final design of the 17.7-mile light-rail system, including a 450-foot three-span balanced-cantilever unit and three 125-foot simple spans, with longitudinal and construction-stage analyses.

I-285 East Express Lanes · Atlanta, Georgia

Supported bridge and structural development for the I-285 East Express Lanes program, a major managed-lane expansion requiring coordinated structural design within a complex metropolitan corridor.

State Route 4 BU/US 1 BU/US 23 BU at CSX · Ware County, Georgia

Performed quality-control review of post-tensioned straddle inverted-T bents for a new grade-separated railroad crossing, including review of refined models, service stresses, flexural capacity, shear, torsion, and corbel design.
