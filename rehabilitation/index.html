REHABILITATION & RETROFITTING

Targeted intervention for aging bridges, difficult constraints, and fast-moving repair needs.

Rehabilitation work often sits at the intersection of condition assessment, refined analysis, constructability, durability, and schedule pressure. The projects below show compact examples of how bridge rehabilitation can range from emergency response and bearing replacement to seismic retrofit, orthotropic deck work, and research-driven system restoration.

REHABILITATION THEMES

Recurring engineering questions behind the project work

Across rehabilitation assignments, the core questions are often the same: what deficiency is driving the problem, how does the existing structure actually behave, what temporary and permanent load paths must be checked, and what intervention remains durable and buildable under active-traffic conditions? The work below highlights these themes through project-specific applications.

01

Load transfer & temporary conditions

Alternative load paths, jacking, temporary support, and staged force transfer frequently govern rehabilitation feasibility.

02

Refined analysis for existing structures

Finite element modeling, seismic analysis, and diagnostic load testing improve confidence where structural behavior is not adequately represented by simplified methods.

03

Durability with minimal disruption

Effective rehabilitation balances long-term performance with traffic management, access limitations, and rapid return to service.

Henry Hudson Bridge

New York, New York

Substructure and foundation modification design to create an alternative load path around ASR-affected existing concrete, using micropiles, a new cast-in-place transfer cap, and refined bridge analysis.

  • Role: Substructure and foundation modification design for alternative load transfer around existing ASR-affected concrete.

Throgs Neck Bridge

New York, New York

Seismic retrofitting and approach-span rehabilitation, including CSI Bridge elastic spectrum and nonlinear time-history analysis, soil-structure interaction, isolation-bearing modeling, and orthotropic deck involvement for the suspended span.

PROJECT 06

Sonovoid Bridge Rehabilitation

Broward County, Florida

Research-led rehabilitation of a prestressed concrete voided slab bridge, combining diagnostic load testing, refined analysis, NSM stainless-steel dowels, and CFRP strengthening to restore transverse load distribution with minimal traffic disruption.

  • Role: Research, analysis, field validation, and rehabilitation concept development for Broward County Sonovoid bridge systems.
  • Focus: Developed and validated the rehabilitation concept using NSM stainless-steel dowels and externally bonded CFRP.
