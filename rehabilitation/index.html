REHABILITATION & RETROFITTING

Rehabilitation work often sits at the intersection of condition assessment, refined analysis, constructability, durability, and schedule pressure. The projects below show compact examples of how bridge rehabilitation can range from emergency response and bearing replacement to seismic retrofit, orthotropic deck work, and research-driven system restoration.

SELECTED PROJECTS

Bridge rehabilitation across a wide range of structures

Jiayi’s rehabilitation experience spans major long-span and complex bridges as well as smaller local structures located over critical highways and transportation corridors. His work has included seismic retrofit, alternative load-path design, bearing replacement, orthotropic deck replacement, emergency repair, collision-damage repair, strengthening, and field-validated rehabilitation—often under demanding traffic, staging, access, and schedule constraints.
