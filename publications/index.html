Structures Congress 2026 · ASCE · Pages 118–132

Design of Complex Bridge Substructure Using the Strut-and-Tie Method

Jiayi Ding, Kishan Singh, Quan Do, and David Konz

This paper presents a case study of inverted-T and hammerhead pier caps that behave as deep beams. It compares strut-and-tie modeling with refined analytical methods to evaluate load paths, stress transfer, detailing, and practical design implications for complex bridge substructures.

Soil–Structure Interaction Analysis of Balanced Cantilever Segmental Bridges

Jiayi Ding, Porter Hawkins, Quan Do, and Daniel Castillo

A case study of an independent design review for a four-span post-tensioned cast-in-place segmental bridge in Colorado. The work integrates construction-stage analysis, creep and shrinkage, moment–curvature-based stiffness, and foundation stiffness derived from soil–structure interaction analysis.

2024

International Bridge Conference 2024 · San Antonio, Texas

A Novel Rehabilitation Solution for Precast Concrete Slab Beam Bridges

Jiayi Ding and Antonio Nanni

This paper documents the development and validation of a rehabilitation strategy for precast concrete slab beam bridges with deteriorated shear keys. The method combines near-surface-mounted dowel reinforcement and externally bonded CFRP to improve lateral load distribution and restore more monolithic structural behavior while reducing traffic impacts during construction.

Materials · Volume 17, Issue 5 · Article 1171

Damage Detection in FRP-Reinforced Concrete Elements

Pranit Malla, Seyed Saman Khedmatgozar Dolati, Jesus D. Ortiz, Armin B. Mehrabi, Antonio Nanni, and Jiayi Ding

This peer-reviewed study evaluates ground-penetrating radar and phased-array ultrasonic methods for detecting damage in FRP-reinforced concrete elements. The experimental program examines how reinforcement type, depth, diameter, and simulated defects affect the capabilities and limitations of these nondestructive testing techniques.

Bridge Engineering Institute Conference 2023 · Rome, Italy

Diagnostic Load Testing of a Bridge Before and After Rehabilitation

Jiayi Ding, Nicolas Begasse, Adriana Toro, Francisco De Caso, and Antonio Nanni

This paper reports pre- and post-rehabilitation diagnostic load testing of a prestressed concrete voided slab bridge in Broward County, Florida. Field measurements were used to quantify lateral load distribution and structural response, providing experimental evidence to evaluate the effectiveness of the rehabilitation intervention.
