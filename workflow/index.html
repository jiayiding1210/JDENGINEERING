INTEGRATED DIGITAL WORKFLOW

Connecting specialized engineering platforms into one coordinated analysis and design process.

Efficient delivery of complex bridge projects often depends on using multiple well-developed software platforms for the tasks they perform best—and connecting them so information can move reliably from modeling through design and engineering decision-making.

WHY IT MATTERS

Use proven tools where they are strongest, then connect the workflow.

Jiayi develops workflows around established structural analysis and design platforms such as OpenBridge, CSI-Bridge, LARSA 4D, FB-MultiPier, ANSYS, LEAP Bridge, MDX, and AASHTOWare. The objective is not to force every task into a single software environment, but to understand the strengths and limitations of each platform and use them strategically for specialized components of the design.

Customized tools and automation are then used to connect these platforms, helping reduce repetitive manual work, maintain consistency between analysis stages, and improve the transparency of complex engineering calculations.

FROM INPUT TO ENGINEERING DECISION

A workflow built around structural behavior—not software output alone.

The workflow supports both preprocessing and post-processing so engineers can move from raw model inputs and large result sets to clear, reviewable design decisions.

01

Model & Loading Preparation

Automating geometry, loading, input organization, and repetitive preprocessing tasks to improve consistency and reduce setup time across analytical models.

02

Multi-Platform Integration

Connecting specialized software so each platform can be used where its analytical or design capability adds the most value to the project.

03

Result Post-Processing

Transforming large volumes of analysis output into organized summaries, visualizations, envelopes, and demand-to-capacity information that engineers can review efficiently.

04

Behavior & Vulnerability Insight

Using processed results to understand load paths, structural response, critical demands, and potential vulnerabilities rather than relying on isolated output values.

05

Design Coordination

Maintaining a clearer connection between analysis, component design, detailing, and multidisciplinary coordination as design decisions evolve.

06

Informed Engineering Judgment

Presenting results in a form that supports technical review, comparison of alternatives, quality control, and informed decisions by the broader project team.

DELIVERY PRINCIPLE

Digital integration should make complex engineering more understandable, not more opaque.

The value of an integrated workflow is not simply faster computation. It is the ability to combine proven engineering platforms, automation, and clear post-processing while preserving traceability, technical rigor, and engineering judgment throughout project delivery.
