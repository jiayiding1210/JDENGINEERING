FLORIDA BRIDGE COMPETITION

Helping students understand structures by building bridges

FDOT District 4 · Florida

Jiayi volunteered at the Florida Bridge Competition hosted by FDOT District 4, working with students as they explored structural engineering through hands-on bridge building. The event created an opportunity to share practical engineering knowledge, discuss how bridge systems carry load, and help participants connect classroom concepts with real structural behavior.

By guiding students through their bridge models and answering questions about structural behavior, design choices, and constructability, the volunteer team helped make engineering more approachable and tangible for the next generation of engineers.
