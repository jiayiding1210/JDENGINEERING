SERVICE & RECOGNITION

Professional contribution beyond project delivery

Jiayi contributes to the structural engineering profession through technical knowledge sharing, peer review, professional service, university engagement, leadership development, and participation in the broader ASCE and SEI community.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICE

Contributing to the engineering community

Jiayi is an active member of the American Society of Civil Engineers and the Structural Engineering Institute. His professional contributions include technical presentations, knowledge sharing with practitioners and students, peer review for the ASCE Journal of Bridge Engineering, and industry engagement with the University of Illinois Civil & Environmental Engineering community.

He has also participated in leadership development through the ASCE Younger Member Leadership Program and supports the dissemination of emerging technologies and state-of-the-art practices in structural engineering.

TECHNICAL COMMITTEE SERVICE

Bridge & Tunnel Maintenance, Inspection, & Rehabilitation

Jiayi serves as a voting member of the ASCE Structural Engineering Institute's Bridge & Tunnel Maintenance, Inspection, & Rehabilitation Committee. The committee advances and disseminates knowledge related to the effective management of bridge and tunnel inventories, including inspection, repair, rehabilitation, information systems, software, funding strategies, and decision-making approaches.

This service aligns closely with Jiayi's professional interests in bridge assessment, maintenance, rehabilitation, nondestructive evaluation, structural monitoring, and lifecycle asset management.

2026 RECOGNITION

SEI Futures Fund Young Professional Scholarship

In February 2026, the Structural Engineering Institute announced its Structures Congress 2026 scholarship recipients supported by the SEI Futures Fund. Jiayi Ding, P.E., M.ASCE, was named among the Young Professional recipients.

The 2026 scholarship class included 50 students, 28 young professionals, and 12 future leaders representing 31 states and 6 countries. According to SEI, the scholarships provide financial assistance so recipients can attend committee meetings, connect with SEI leadership, and more fully participate in Structures Congress and the SE 2050 Summit.
