ABOUT

Engineering depth, practical judgment, and continuous improvement.

Jiayi Ding, PhD, PE, PMP, is a structural engineer and technical manager focused on bridge and transportation infrastructure across the full project and asset lifecycle.

ENGINEERING FOCUS

From structural behavior to long-term infrastructure performance.

His work focuses on the analysis, design, and maintenance of bridges and transportation structures, with particular emphasis on structural mechanics, constructability, nondestructive testing, rehabilitation, and experimental investigations into design methodologies that improve reliability, performance, and service life.

His technical experience spans conventional and complex bridge systems, advanced structural analysis, substructure and foundation behavior, load effects, and rehabilitation strategies. He approaches engineering problems by combining analytical rigor with practical judgment, constructability, and a clear understanding of how structural decisions affect project delivery and long-term performance.

DIGITAL & EXPERIMENTAL APPROACH

Connecting analysis, field evidence, and digital tools.

Jiayi has contributed to emerging digital approaches in transportation infrastructure, including nondestructive testing, field investigation, diagnostic load testing, digital twins, and data-informed asset management.

He also develops integrated engineering workflows that connect established structural software with customized preprocessing, data transfer, post-processing, visualization, and decision-support tools. The goal is to use proven platforms efficiently while improving transparency, interpretation of structural behavior, and the quality of engineering decisions.

LEADERSHIP & DELIVERY

Technical leadership grounded in project execution.

In addition to technical design and analysis, his work includes project delivery, quality oversight, multidisciplinary coordination, staff development, and technical leadership. He is particularly interested in improving how engineering teams combine specialized expertise, digital tools, and sound judgment to solve complex infrastructure problems effectively and efficiently.
