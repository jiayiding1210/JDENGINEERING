PROJECT SUMMARY

Sonovoid Bridge Rehabilitation

This project served as both a field implementation and research case study for rehabilitation of Sonovoid/prestressed concrete voided slab bridge systems with deteriorated load-transfer behavior. The work combined diagnostic load testing before and after rehabilitation, refined analysis of structural behavior, and development of a practical strengthening approach using near-surface-mounted stainless-steel dowels and CFRP. The overall concept was tailored to restore more monolithic response while limiting traffic impacts by performing much of the work from below the bridge.

JIAYI'S CONTRIBUTION

Focused technical responsibilities

  • Developed and validated the rehabilitation concept using NSM stainless-steel dowels and externally bonded CFRP.
  • Performed diagnostic load testing before and after rehabilitation to quantify the change in load distribution and structural response.
  • Integrated finite element analysis, field implementation, and publication of project findings through conference papers and research outputs.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

The goal is to restore structural behavior and load sharing, not simply cover visible cracking.

02

Design & constructability

Validation through field measurement is especially valuable for rehabilitation of existing bridge systems with uncertain continuity behavior.

03

Project delivery

Details must remain constructible under active traffic conditions and should favor durable materials and limited disruption.
