PROJECT SUMMARY

Sheridan Street Bridge over the Florida Turnpike

Because Sheridan Street crosses the heavily traveled Florida Turnpike with limited vertical clearance, repeated overheight-vehicle collisions created an urgent need for a rapid and durable repair strategy. Jiayi developed the precast prestressed beam repair, strand repair, and deck repair design. The work was delivered under a compressed schedule, and the bridge was repaired and reopened to traffic within a short two-month window.

JIAYI'S CONTRIBUTION

Focused technical responsibilities

  • Developed the repair design for the impacted precast prestressed beams, including strand repair and deck repair.
  • Evaluated temporary support and staging concepts necessary to implement repairs over a busy and operational corridor.
  • Supported a repair approach that enabled the bridge to be repaired and reopened within a compressed schedule.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

Collision-damage repairs are schedule-driven and must address immediate safety, residual capacity, and constructability over live traffic.

02

Design & constructability

Prestressed member repair requires careful treatment of damaged concrete, strands, and force-transfer zones.

03

Project delivery

Temporary support, maintenance of traffic, and night work frequently govern the practicability of the chosen repair strategy.
