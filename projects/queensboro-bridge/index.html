PROJECT SUMMARY

Queensboro Bridge

For the landmark historic Queensboro Bridge, Jiayi was involved in the design of the upper roadway replacement using a steel open-rib orthotropic deck. A key engineering decision was the evaluation of open-rib versus closed-rib deck systems. In this case, the final preference was driven primarily by fabrication considerations rather than by a major difference in structural demand, highlighting how constructability and shop production can govern deck-system selection on rehabilitation projects.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

On major rehabilitation projects, fabrication and erection efficiency may be more decisive than marginal analytical differences between alternatives.

02

Design & constructability

Historic-bridge work demands sensitivity to geometry, existing details, and maintenance of traffic throughout replacement phases.

03

Project delivery

Orthotropic deck design must address fatigue-prone details, rib-to-deck welding, and long-term inspectability.
