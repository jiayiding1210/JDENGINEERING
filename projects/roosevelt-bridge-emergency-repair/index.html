PROJECT SUMMARY

Roosevelt Bridge Emergency Repair

Following major distress discovered in June 2020, the Roosevelt Bridge emergency repair required rapid structural evaluation and fast-tracked rehabilitation planning for a critical transportation link. Jiayi performed an independent design review of the proposed repair and rehabilitation design, including the use of external post-tensioning tendons for restoring capacity in the segmental bridge system. The project is widely recognized as a rapid-response preservation effort that restored service under a compressed schedule.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

Emergency bridge repairs must balance speed with a defensible understanding of structural behavior and residual capacity.

02

Design & constructability

For segmental systems, tendon force paths, continuity, and staged behavior are critical when designing or reviewing rapid-strengthening measures.

03

Project delivery

Maintaining public confidence and minimizing closure duration are often key project drivers alongside technical adequacy.
