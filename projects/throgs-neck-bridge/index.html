PROJECT SUMMARY

Throgs Neck Bridge

The work included seismic retrofitting and approach-span rehabilitation for a major suspension bridge crossing. Refined CSI Bridge models were developed for the two approaches to evaluate bridge behavior, substructure load demands, and the system response before and after seismic isolation bearings were introduced. The analysis combined elastic response-spectrum evaluation, nonlinear time-history analysis, soil-structure interaction, and iterative refinement of bearing properties. Jiayi also contributed to closed-rib steel orthotropic deck design for the main suspended span.

JIAYI'S CONTRIBUTION

Focused technical responsibilities

  • Developed and refined CSI Bridge finite element models for the two approach structures.
  • Defined isolation-bearing parameters and performed elastic spectrum, nonlinear time-history, and soil–structure interaction analyses in an iterative design process.
  • Contributed to closed-rib steel orthotropic deck design for the main suspended span and coordinated substructure-demand evaluation after retrofit.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

Isolator behavior, period shift, and demand redistribution need to be understood at the full-system level, not only at individual components.

02

Design & constructability

Approach structures often control practicality of retrofit because bearings, supports, and foundations interact strongly with soil stiffness.

03

Project delivery

Orthotropic deck selection requires coordination between structural behavior, fatigue performance, fabrication, and future maintenance.
