PROJECT SUMMARY

Henry Hudson Bridge

This rehabilitation assignment focused on establishing a reliable alternative load path from the existing bridge arch ribs into a new substructure and foundation system where ASR-related distress in existing substructure concrete needed to be bypassed. The concept relied on micropiles placed around the existing foundation system and a cast-in-place concrete cap over the abutment skewbacks to receive and transfer arch-rib forces into the new foundation elements.

JIAYI'S CONTRIBUTION

Focused technical responsibilities

  • Designed the alternative load path system and the associated micropile-supported foundation modifications.
  • Designed the cast-in-place concrete cap over the existing abutment skewbacks for transferring loads from the arch ribs into the new support system.
  • Developed the bridge model and performed structural analysis to evaluate force transfer and behavior of the rehabilitation concept.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

Load-path continuity and staging are central when introducing a new support system adjacent to an existing arch bridge.

02

Design & constructability

Micropile layout, skewback geometry, and the interface between new and existing concrete govern both constructability and force transfer.

03

Project delivery

Alternative load-path schemes must be checked not only for final demands, but also for temporary conditions during transfer.
