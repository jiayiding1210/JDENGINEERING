PROJECT SUMMARY

Miami International Airport People Mover Bridge Rehabilitation

This rehabilitation assignment focused on an active airport people-mover bridge where disk bearings required replacement and the steel structure required rehabilitation. Jiayi developed the finite element model used to evaluate bridge behavior, bearing load demands, and the jacking forces required to lift the bridge for bearing replacement. A central challenge was maintaining train operations during the work, which placed a premium on staging, serviceability control, and operational coordination.

JIAYI'S CONTRIBUTION

Focused technical responsibilities

  • Developed the bridge finite element model to evaluate bridge behavior during rehabilitation and bearing replacement.
  • Calculated bearing load demands and required jacking forces for replacing disk bearings.
  • Supported a rehabilitation strategy that maintained people-mover operations while controlling structural response during temporary conditions.

KEY CONSIDERATIONS

Engineering issues that shaped the work

The project required a targeted response to structural behavior, staging, and constructability. These considerations summarize the main themes that governed decision-making.

01

Structural focus

Operational structures often require temporary works to be designed around tight displacement tolerances and limited service interruption.

02

Design & constructability

Bearing replacement is governed by both permanent design demands and temporary jacking/load-transfer conditions.

03

Project delivery

Airport projects require close coordination between structural engineering, operations, access, and safety constraints.
